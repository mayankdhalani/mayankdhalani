Chapter one / About

A person of
many interests.

I'm interested in what makes us human — how we heal, how we feel, and how we make meaning out of ordinary days. I don't think those interests are as far apart as they seem.

01 — The science

Medicine & research

I'm a physician working in critical care research at Mayo Clinic in Florida. I care about asking better questions and finding answers that matter to patients.

02 — The words

Poetry & literature

Some thoughts don't arrive as neat conclusions. I write poetry and reflections to give those thoughts a place to land.

03 — The elsewhere

Everything in between

Mountain trails, books, music, art, and conversations that run longer than planned. These are some of my favourite ways to stay curious.

Chapter two / The writing room

A home for

Poems, fragments, and reflections — a collection of things I've tried to understand by putting them into words. For now, they live on my WordPress blog; soon they'll have their own space here.
