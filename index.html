Chapter one / About

A person of
many interests.

I'm interested in what makes us human — how we heal, what art makes us feel, and how stories help us find meaning in ordinary days. To me, those worlds have always belonged together.

01 — The science

Medicine & research

I'm a physician and researcher at Mayo Clinic in Florida. I care about asking better questions, exploring new ideas, and finding answers that matter to patients.

02 — The words

Poetry & literature

Some thoughts don't arrive as neat conclusions. I write poetry and reflections to give those thoughts a place to land.

03 — The art of noticing

Art & the everyday

I love art for its ability to express what words sometimes cannot. Add books, music, mountain trails, and conversations that run longer than planned, and you have some of my favourite ways to stay curious.
